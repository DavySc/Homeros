The two differences are not colour bugs:

- **In-buffer `#+TODO:` keywords** (`todo-line.org`): `#+TODO: REVIEW | DONE` makes REVIEW a keyword in Org and not here. Build B already owes this for the parser; the PR's list of known differences should name it for the colours too.
- **`#+STARTUP: overview`** (`overview.org`, reduced from ORG-NEWS): with the buffer folded, batch Emacs leaves the newline of a `#+end_src` line before a folded headline without the block-line face. Without the STARTUP line the same text agrees. This is the oracle's folding, not font-lock's rule.

## 2. Finding 1: a long line breaks the frame gate (required before `.org` is switched on)

`fontify` colours a whole line on every `orgfx_faces` call, and `src/core/draw.c` asks for a line's faces 256 bytes at a time, so one visible line costs (line / 256) calls of the whole line. Measured with `perf.c` (gcc -O2, median and range of 5 runs, calls as the core makes them over the first 8 KB of the line):

| One line of | 2 KB | 4 KB | 8 KB | 32 KB | 128 KB |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| plain words | 0.33 ms (0.31 to 0.39) | 1.37 (1.27 to 2.21) | 5.16 (5.11 to 5.17) | 19.7 (19.6 to 24.1) | 82.5 (82.1 to 86.0) |
| `<2026-01-01 x ` repeated, never closed | 1.41 (1.32 to 1.50) | 9.56 (9.23 to 10.3) | 70.0 (66.1 to 73.3) | 981 (842 to 1,010) | |
| `<%%(x) ` repeated, never closed | 1.48 (1.44 to 1.75) | 10.1 (9.87 to 10.7) | 71.1 (70.5 to 72.3) | 1,072 (996 to 1,157) | |

The gate is 2 ms for the whole frame. A paragraph kept on one line (no hard wrap) of 4 to 8 KB is ordinary Org text, so the first row is the one that matters; the other two are quadratic in the line on top (`ts_both` and the `<%%(` search each run to the line's end from every opener).

The colours are not reachable from the editor until package 12 adds the `.org` dispatch to `syntax.c`, so this does not block the merge. It must be fixed before that dispatch. The author answered on 2026-11-19 18:33 UTC that the fix is ready locally (a line's faces kept between calls, both scans stopping once nothing later can close; a 32 KB line of words 75 ms to 1.9 ms by its measurement), will come as a PR after this one merges, and that it holds the dispatch request until then. That PR gets its own review.

Other costs, for the record (same probe, a 900 KB text, one byte typed at the end, then the last 4 KB drawn):

| Text | `orgfx_update` | 4 KB of faces after it |
|---|---|---|
| a headline every 50 lines | 0.009 ms (0.008 to 0.010) | 0.134 (0.131 to 0.140) |
| no headline, plain lines | 0.838 (0.777 to 0.846) | 0.366 (0.328 to 0.368) |
| no headline, one list of 60,000 items | 12.9 (12.4 to 13.0) | 0.627 (0.588 to 0.651) |

The last row is `org_edit` reparsing the one list (PR 45's behaviour, now on the key path, where `stop` is not asked). The PR body's bound for the worst update ("about 4.5 ms on 902 KB") holds for the manual, not for every text of that size.

`tools/org_conform.py` runs `main()` when imported; a `__main__` guard would let other scripts use its generator.

## 5. By reading

- `orgfx_edit` keeps one pending range in old and new coordinates; the cases (before, inside, after, overlapping the pending range) come out right by hand and under the fuzzer's plants.
- `orgfx_faces` does not allocate; `orgfx_update` grows one copy of the text and never shrinks it.
- A scan starts at the headline before the window or continues the kept one. The kept scan is only a saving: starting fresh at the headline gives the same colours (the fuzzer compares windows of any size and order with the whole text).
