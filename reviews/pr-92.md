## Note (not blocking, older than this PR)

A spill takes its symbol's colours whole, reversed or not, and only the cursor is asked of
the cell under it. So where ATTR_REV differs between the symbol and the blank after it the
spill is drawn in the blank's own background colour: **measured** (cur2, both columns) ⟹
with ATTR_REV and a plain blank shows 0 pixels of spill, and 57 only with the cursor on the
blank; ⟹ plain with an ATTR_REV blank the same. Main's wl.c fails these rows too (it shows
the spill only with the cursor on the symbol). So README's "the cursor neither hides nor
bares any part" does not hold there. Reach (hypothesis, from grep): small today, since
ATTR_REV runs (status line, picker's chosen row, popup menu) cover the blank too and the
Visual area is a face (passes). A fix would ask "reversed?" of the cell under the part for
ATTR_REV as for the cursor.
