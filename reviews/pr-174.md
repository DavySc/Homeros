# Review of PR 174: Lean's long-lines test compares a line 4 times as long

Reviewed head: bac2729a68eb8ec3cbd5e6f73fbdc1c74d7e3257 (one commit on main eafbdd33, current main on 2026-11-27 18:21 UTC).
Reviewer: a separate review thread, briefed with the diff and the PR body, not with the author's conclusions.
Machine: a 4-core cloud container. Every time is CPU time under ASan and UBSan at -O1 (the Makefile's test flags). A "ratio" is what the test computes for one of its six lines: the least of 5 tries at 4n bytes over the least of 5 tries at n bytes; the test fails at 8 or more. A "run" is one process of the test (or of the probe, the same test with every try printed, `pr-174-repro/mkprobe.py`).

## Verdict

**Changes requested at bac2729a.** One thing:

1. **The clean head fails falsely on a loaded machine: 3 of 1,061 runs** (measured, finding 1). Always the `trace[` line. Its clean ratio is 5.3, not 4, because it still has the cost this PR removed from comments: `bracket()` reads the byte at the remembered run's end, the line's end, then the next token reads back at its own place, so past 4 KB `tsql_byte` refills its window 1.3 times per byte (26,382 refills on the 19,900-byte line; 50 or fewer on the other five). That leaves 1.5 times of room below the bound where the other lines have 2, and the refills make that line slower beside other work. A one-line stand-in for the fix puts the line at 4.0 and had 0 false failures in 660 loaded runs.

What is good and should stay:

- The ratio does what the PR says for plants: all 6 plants the ratio is for were caught in every one of 1,362 runs, idle and loaded: the five quadratic ones (600 runs) at 15.1 to 17.3 times, the new one (762 runs) at 10.1 to 15.4. (The PR body counts six old ones; plant 123, no budget, is stopped by the state check, not by a ratio.)
- The `at()` change is right and changes no colour: 0 of 847,952 bytes in 619 cases differ from VS Code (every run above repeats this).
- No test-only hook in product code, and the test still takes 1.7 s.

## 1. No false reds on a noisy runner? Not yet (measured)

The clean head's probe, `battery.sh` and `rr.sh`:

| Load | runs | failed | `trace[` ratio: least, median, most | most on any other line |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| idle | 400 | 0 | 4.77, 5.28, 7.55 | 5.06 |
| 4 copies at once | 400 | 2 (8.12, 8.74) | 3.72, 5.3, 8.74 | 7.05 |
| 8 copies at once | 200 | 1 (8.48) | 3.30, 5.37, 8.48 | 6.41 |
| beside 8 busy loops (4 of them walking 64 MB) | 61 | 0 | 4.89, 5.29, 7.19 | 4.78 |

In the three failures the short side was as fast as ever (5.0 to 5.8 ms) and all five long tries were slow (42.7 to 49.4 ms against 26 idle). Over the 600 loaded runs, the five other lines' ratio left its median by 1.5 times or more 5 times in 3,000 and by 2 times never; `trace[` needs only 1.5.

Why `trace[` sits at 5.3 (measured, `scale.c` with a counter in `tsql_byte`, 5 tries per length):

| line | refills at n | refills at 4n |
|---|---|---|
| backticks in a comment | 1 | 1 |
| links | 322 | 698 |
| `trace[` | 1,494 | 26,382 |
| `def {` | 20 | 50 |
| `/-!-/` | 12 | 30 |
| blanks in a doc comment | 1 | 38 |

Doubling `trace[` from 1,244 to 19,900 bytes costs 1.97, 2.23, 2.37 and 2.36 times. With `tsql.h`'s window at 64 KB (an experiment, not a proposal) the line's ratio is 4.00.

The stand-in fix, to show the cause and the cure, not to prescribe it: in `bracket()`, `return e < x->eol && tsql_byte(x->r, e) == ']' ? e + 1 : 0;`. With it the worst clean ratio of the whole test is 4.1, the oracle still shows 0 bytes differing, and:

| Load | runs | failed | `trace[` ratio: least, median, most | most on any line |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 copies at once | 400 | 0 | 2.76, 4.00, 5.28 | 6.28 |
| 8 copies at once | 200 | 0 | 3.72, 4.01, 4.34 | 5.06 |
| beside 8 busy loops | 60 | 0 | 3.64, 4.02, 4.64 | 5.50 |

**Asked for:** remove that cost from the `trace[` path (or from `tsql_byte`, if one place can serve both this and `at()`), with a plant that undoes it, as the PR did for comments. The plant needs a row whose short side is under the window, as the blanks row is (the `trace[` row's short side is already past it).

`tools/planted.sh` itself on lines 137 and 140 (a tree with only those two lines in `tests/plants/`, 1 run, beside the busy loops): both `caught`, by `CHECK(ncase && !bad && worst < 8)`.

Plants of mine (`variant.sh NAME FILE OLD NEW`, 1 run each):

| plant | result |
|---|---|
| x2: `ws()` remembers only a run's start (`if (p == m->wb)`) | caught, blanks at 15.86 |
| x3: `unbracket()` forgets at every 12th byte | caught, `trace[` at 16.08 |

x1 is the limit of any ratio with this bound: a quadratic cost smaller than the linear one at n bytes passes. Lines over 20,000 units are plain, so what passes is at most about 2.5 times the clean time on the longest line read. Not asked to change; the comment could say it.

The new plant's room is the thinnest, 1.26 times at its lowest (10.09 against 8), because it is not quadratic: it is 3 times the cost past 4 KB. 0 escapes in 762 runs, so not blocking. It also depends on `tsql.h`'s 4,096: a larger window turns the plant into NOT CAUGHT, which a train would show.

## 3. Smaller things (not blocking)

- **The backticks row is never the check that fails** (measured, x4): plant 123 is stopped by the state check before the rows, and without that check the links row catches it too. Either drop the row (shorter) or leave it; no plant reaches it.
- **Two ways to take CPU time and the least of 5 in `tests/`**: `test_org.c` has `cpu_ms()` and `least()` (PR 143), this test has `clock()` and its own minimum. `make dupes` does not look in `tests/`. One helper in `tests/check.h` (thread 0's file) would serve both.
- **A planted run is slow**: 1 min 49 s for plant 135, 35 s for 138, 20 s for 136 (10 tries of quadratic code). The old test stopped after one.
- The PR body gives the clean lines as "at most 5.4 to 5.5 times" without saying why a linear line is not at 4; finding 1 is that reason.
