## The first pass (`0541d6e`) and what became of it

`make check` passed once on `0541d6e` (main `1bb9360`), including `make win`. Three asks:

1. **Blocking: recent files kept `/private/` and `/TMP/` paths that Neovim drops.** The PR had cut shada.c's `removable()` because "no other spelling of /tmp/ reaches it; /private/ is macOS-only". On Linux, Neovim 0.12's default 'shada' is `!,'100,<50,s10,h,r/tmp/,r/private/` (`nvim --clean`), and `shada_removable()` matches it ignoring case. Measured with `oldfiles.py`: Neovim kept only `c.txt` (3 of 3), homeros at `0541d6e` kept all three (3 of 3).
   **Fixed:** `shada_removable()` is back as one line of two `strncasecmp`, and `test_shada.c` calls it directly on `/private/x.sql`, `/TMP/y.sql`, `/tmp/z.sql` and three near misses. The three shada plants (call removed, `/private/` dropped, `strncasecmp` to `strncmp`) are in `tests/plants/shada.txt`. The test needs no directories made as root, so CI's workflow is unchanged (the author had proposed `sudo mkdir /private /TMP`; decided against it at 08:05 UTC).
2. **Duplication now removable:** PR 66 put `grid_puts()`/`grid_width()` in grid.h; `dash.c`'s `str()` and `width()` copied them. **Fixed:** `str()` calls `grid_puts`, `width()` is gone, the footer uses `grid_width`.
3. **A test block that checked nothing of its own:** the "another editor wrote the file" block in `test_shada.c` could not reach `SAVE_KEPT`. The only plant I found that it caught (y1, `file.c` leaving the swapped-out copy) is caught by `test_file_save.c:372` too (y2); `pr-73-repro/my-plants.txt`, log `my-plants-0541d6e.log`. **Fixed:** deleted, with `<dirent.h>`.
