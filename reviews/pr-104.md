## What it misses

- **measured** (`variants.c`, `variants.sh`): 11 ordinary ways to write the same mechanisms, compiled with `-Wall -Wextra -Werror` and placed in `src/tools/`: UTF-8 with the mask on the left (`(c & 0x3F) | 0x80`), with `128 +`, with `c % 64 + 0x80`; a line splitter with `== '\n'`, with `memchr`, with `*p != '\n'`; digits as `10 * n +`, as `n *= 10; n += `, with `- 48`, with `strtol`; a cursor jump that sets the fields without `core_set_want`. **0 of 11 are flagged.** The telltales are fitted to the copies that were on main. That is the nature of a telltale and the PR says "telltale", but CLAUDE.md's new sentence does not say it (change 3).
- **measured** (`shape.c`): the renamed pass misses a copy with one statement moved (as CLAUDE.md says) and a copy whose only renamed local is one upper-case letter (`P` to `Q`: upper-case names are kept). It flags two functions that differ only in `true`/`false` or in the lower-case global they touch; I count those as copies worth folding, so no change asked.
- **measured**: digits are also parsed by `strtol`, `strtoul`, `atoi` and `sscanf` in about 25 lines of git, keys, infoview, lsp and syntax, next to `match_num`. number-parse sees only the hand loop. Whether libc and `match_num` are "two ways" is a question for the owners, not this PR.

## Changes asked

1. **Read each file once.** The owned pass calls `clean(f)` for every rule and every file: 9 x 93 reads and comment passes, on top of the one in `tokens`. CPU time (`cpu.py`, 5 alternating runs, user+sys, while a `make check` ran beside it): old tool 1.13 s (1.07 to 1.22), PR 1.66 s (1.55 to 1.81), reading once 1.36 s (1.29 to 1.48). The ranges do not overlap. `clean-once.diff` is the 4-line change (one line more than now); its output is byte-identical (41 lines with the known list emptied). `passes.py` times the passes apart: owned as written 0.61 s, on text cleaned once 0.13 s. (The PR's own figure, 1.89 s against 1.17 s wall, agrees.)
2. **utf8-encode misses the usual operand order.** `0x80 *(\+|\|(?!\|))|(?<!\|)\| *0x80\b` also sees `(c & 0x3F) | 0x80` and adds 0 findings on the head and on the 4 PRs above that touch `src/` most (**measured**). `+ 0x80` on the right is left out on purpose: it would hit the rounding in wl.c:615.
3. **Say what the owned pass cannot see, and fix one reason.** CLAUDE.md's "what the check cannot see" list should gain "a mechanism written without its telltale". The docstring says the programs in `src/*/*/` "link none of" the modules; **measured**, 8 of the 9 include module headers (`lsp/bench/stale.c` and `wl/test/bench.c` include `loop.h` and call `clock_gettime` all the same). Skipping them is right (they are benches, probes and the infoview server: separate programs, like `tests/`, and they would add 12 owned findings and 3 renamed pairs); the stated reason is not.

## Notes (no change asked)

- Owned findings are keyed `name file`, one per matching line. Re-wrapping a listed copy changes the count and fails the check until the list is edited; a second copy in a listed file can take the place of a folded one. A per-file key would be shorter but would let a second copy in a listed file pass, so per line is the stricter choice.
- When PR 101 folds git/keys.c:248 and infoview/scan.c:99 (asked by the author), the six `utf8-encode` lines must leave `tests/dupes-known.txt` in the same merge, or the check fails with "no longer copied".
- Like PR 66, this is a repo-wide check: a package that lands a telltale first breaks it. None of the six open PRs does today (above).
- The files are thread 0's (`tools/`, `tests/`, CLAUDE.md); thread 0's OK is the author's to bring.
- Code size: `tools/dupes.py` goes from 59 to 106 lines, of which 14 are the docstring and comments. I found nothing else to cut: the three passes share the hit list and the known list, and no Makefile line changed.
