# Review of PR 68: wl: ligatures keep their left half

Reviewed head c92ffc8 (fb07cb0 merged with main d4646c4), 2026-11-18. Reviewer: a separate
Fable 5.2 thread. Probes and how to build them: `reviews/pr-68-repro/`.

**Verdict: changes requested.** The fix does what it says on a whole redraw, but the wider clip
leaves wrong pixels beside 214 symbols when only part of a row is redrawn (finding 1, new with
this PR), and the PR's own headline case, `x /\ y`, still draws unjoined when it is typed
(finding 2, older than this PR, same cause on screen). Both are small to fix.

## What was confirmed

- **measured** (1 run each side, `probe-lig`): with the cursor elsewhere, the `-` cell of `a->b`
  has 18 pixels of ink with the PR and 0 with the old line. The bug and the fix are real.
- **measured**: `make test TESTS=wl` passes 3 of 3 runs on the head.
- **measured** (1 run): the plant "ligature cut at its own cell" fails the test at
  `tests/test_wl.c:356`.
- **measured** (`bear.c`, 16, 20, 24, 32 px): of 8,836 ASCII pairs, 78 glyphs reach back more than
  1 px, and none reaches into the cell before the pair. So a ligature stays inside its own word.

## Findings

### 1. Blocking: a symbol that starts left of its cell is drawn differently by a partial redraw (new)

The comment and the README say "span keeps both inside lo..hi". For a leftward spill that is not
so: `span()` starts at a word's first cell, and the glyph of that first cell is now cut at `lo`
there, while a whole redraw (`lo` = 0) lets it reach into the blank before it.

- **measured** (`bear.c`): 214 of 4,578 single glyphs (Latin, Greek, U+2000 to U+2BFF, the maths
  alphabets, emoji) start 1 to 4 px left of their cell at 16 px, among them ↔ (U+2194), ⇔ (U+21D4),
  ⟷ (U+27F7), all usual in Lean after a blank.
- **measured** (`probe-lig`, 1 run each; both buffers hold the old row first): the screen differs
  from a whole redraw with the PR and not with the old line for
  `" a"` then `" ↔"`, `" ↔"` then `" a"` (5 pixels of the old ↔ stay in the blank cell),
  `" ⇔ b"` then `" a b"`, `"x ↔"` then `"x a"`.
- **measured**: a whole redraw of `a↔` now puts 4 pixels of ↔ over the `a` (64 against 60 pixels in
  that cell), `a⌫` 7. Blended, so nothing is erased; foot does the same. Not a finding by itself,
  but the README row should say it, since a spill to the right is still "cut at a character".

A fix that passed the probe (`sketch.diff`, **measured**: `probe-lig` 14 of 14 "same", 1 run):
cut at the start of the glyph's word, the mirror of the loop that finds `e`:
`int w0 = c; while (w0 > lo && !blank(&x[w0 - 1])) w0--;`. A ligature still reaches back over its
run; a word's first glyph is cut at its own cell, as before the PR. The author may find a shorter one.

### 2. Blocking for the PR's claim: `/\` and `\/` typed in a row are not joined until a whole redraw (older)

JetBrains Mono joins `/\` and `\/` only with a blank on both sides.

- **measured** (`ctx.c`): `x /\ y` and ` /\ y` shape as a 14 px glyph at left -7; `/\ y`, `x /\`
  and `/\` shape as two 8 px glyphs. `->`, `=>`, `<=`, `!=`, `|-`, `::`, `<->` shape the same with
  and without the blank before.
- `span()` widens a changed span "to the start of a word after a blank, so the span shapes as the
  whole row would" (README). It starts at the `/`, so the run has no blank before it.
- **measured** (`probe-lig`, PR and old line alike): `"x /a y"` then `"x /\ y"` differs from a whole
  redraw (139 pixels in cells 2 and 3), and so does `x /\ y` typed one character at a time with the
  cursor after it. So what Davy types shows `/\` unjoined, and it changes shape at the next whole
  redraw.

This is the symptom the PR sets out to fix, so it belongs here or in a PR right behind it. The
sketch's other line passed the probe: when `lo` > 0, put the blank before the span into the shaped
text (not into the fill), one line.

### 3. Follow-up, not blocking: the cursor on a ligature hides its left half

The whole glyph takes the colours of its last cell.

- **measured** (`probe-lig`, `a->b`): with the cursor on the `>`, the `-` cell has 0 pixels of ink
  (the glyph is drawn in the background colour over a background cell); with the cursor on the `-`,
  the cell is a solid block. Elsewhere 18 pixels. The same must hold for two faces inside one
  ligature (**hypothesis**, not run).
- kitty draws each cell of a ligature in that cell's colours. Before the PR the left half was
  missing everywhere, so this is not a regression; package 8 owes it.

### 4. The tests do not see findings 1 and 2

- The new test draws the ligatures once, after `grid_clear`, so only one path is checked. A case
  that changes a row to and from ` ↔` and `x /\ y` and compares with a whole redraw would fail
  today; each needs its plant.
- **measured**: the random screenshot check does not find them either: `pix2.c` (the package 8
  probe with a pool of ligature characters and six left-reaching symbols), seeds 1 to 5, 100
  frames each, checked every frame: 0 differ for the PR, the old line and the sketch. It frees
  `shown` at each check, so the next frame lands on a fresh buffer; the test's check has `-` and
  `>` but neither `/\` nor a symbol that starts left of its cell.
