## What was checked

- **Correctness of the block skip.** Entering the leaf, `1 <= l <=` the leaf's newline count. The first loop keeps "the l-th remaining '\n' is at or after `i`" and reads only `b[i .. i+31]` with more than 32 bytes left; the second loop then finds it within the leaf's bytes. `> 32` where `>= 32` would also do costs at most 32 single steps in a last block of exactly 32 bytes (reasoned, then run:).
- `reviews/pr-28-repro/edges.c` under ASan and UBSan: 12,543,400 line starts agree with a byte-at-a-time model (measured, 1 run). Leaves of every size from 0 to 1,016 bytes and texts to 2,200 bytes with all newlines, none, only the last byte, only the first byte of the last block, bytes 33 and 32 from the end; a '\n' every 1 to 70 bytes at every phase for lengths 1 to 140 and 990 to 1,100; 300 rounds of 200 random inserts and deletes so that leaves have arbitrary sizes.
- `reviews/pr-28-repro/mutants.sh`: 6 bugs planted in the new two lines (`>=` for `>`, step 31, step 33, count of 31, count past the leaf's bytes, count not subtracted). `edges` and `test_text_rope` catch 6 of 6, `test_text` 5 of 6. A seventh change (`> 64`) only affects speed and is rightly not caught (measured, 1 run each).
- **Regression test.** `test_text_rope` on the old `text.c` under ASan fails `CHECK(dense < 4 * sparse)` 5 of 5 times; on the new code it passes 20 of 20 while `make check` was loading the machine, the ratio between 0.59 and 1.19 against a limit of 4 (measured). It uses CPU time and a ratio, and adds about 1.5 s. Its plant is caught.
- **Speed claim.** Empty lines 51 times faster with gcc and 67 times with clang here (PR: 49 times); 50-byte lines inside the spread, as claimed (measured, table above).
- **`make check`** on the PR head: exit 0, everything including `frama` 227 / 227 and all specs (measured, 1 run).
- **`make planted`**: finding 1.
