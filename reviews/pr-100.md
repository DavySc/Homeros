## Findings

1. **Must, open, outside the PR.**  `make win WIN=git`: `lld-link: error:
   undefined symbol: org_todo_step` (measured, 3 runs: ff8a3b8, 3b385ce,
   ab753b3 merged with a0c2bcf).  keys now calls org; `src/git/check.sh`
   line 37 links keys without org.  With `$(pick org)` after `$(pick sqlfmt)`
   the whole check passes (below).
2. **Must, fixed in 3b385ce.**  cit joined the previous command's undo step:
   on "* a b", `$xcit` then `u` gave "* a b", want "* a " (measured, 3 cases:
   after `x`, after `:s`, after `A z Esc`; nvim-orgmode's map is one undo
   step of its own).  Cause: `map()` runs outside `keys_feed()`, which closes
   the step before each key.  Fix: `undo_close()` before the switch in
   `map()`, with test `xcitu` and a plant; the three cases now give "* a "
   (measured, 1 run each).
3. **Should, fixed in 3b385ce.**  The 'redrawtime' test's upper bound was
   3 s against a 2 s budget.  Measured here (4 cores, ASan build, time of the
   400 `keys_hl` calls):

   | build | lines | load | runs | time |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | planted (no budget) | 50 | idle | 3 | 4.03 to 4.29 s |
   | planted (no budget) | 400 | idle | 1 | 34.9 s |
   | PR | 400 | idle | 3 | 2.024 to 2.043 s |
   | PR | 400 | 16 CPU hogs | 5 | 2.10 to 2.18 s |
   | PR | 400 | 64 CPU hogs | 5 | 2.47 to 2.88 s |
   | PR | 400 | 1 core shared with 8 hogs | 5 | 2.23 to 2.38 s |

   So the plant is missed only by a runner about 11 times faster than this
   one (was 1.4 times with 50 lines and 3 s; that is PR 90's flake), and the
   test fails by itself only past 8 s, 2.8 times the worst loaded run
   (measured; that no CI runner is slower is a hypothesis).  The budget is
   checked every 1024 regex steps, so the overshoot does not grow with the
   line count.
